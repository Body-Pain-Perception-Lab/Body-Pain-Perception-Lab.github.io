A PhD position funded by the Independent Research Fund Denmark (DFF) will open soon, with a start date between 1 June and 31 December 2027. The position is part of the project [Pain in the patient's own words: From AI-based information-seeking to language-based assessment](https://dff.dk/hvad-har-vi-stoettet/se-oversigt-over-stoettet-forskning/?GoCaseId=6244-00293A&Bevilling.TitelDK=fardo&Bevilling.VirkemiddelNavnDK=DFF-Forskningsprojekt1&Bevilling.Finanslovsaar=2026&page=1), which focuses on the patient's perspective on pain, from the questions people ask AI to how their experiences can shape pain assessment. Stay tuned for the official call!

If you’re interested in applying for funding to join our lab, please reach out to [Francesca](/people/01_FF/)!
