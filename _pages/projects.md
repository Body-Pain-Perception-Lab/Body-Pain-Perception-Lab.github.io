## **ERC Starting Grant (2021-2026)**  
**Illusions in the thermo-nociceptive system**  
Investigating how the nervous system generates pain-like sensations in absence of noxious stimuli, bridging psychophysics, neuroimaging, and computational modeling.

## **Lundbeckfonden Experiment Grant (2024-2026)**  
**Predicting chronic pain through linguistic analysis of electronic medical records**  
Using natural language processing and statistical models to detect early signs of chronic pain from doctors’ notes, narratives and patient records.

## **NAD fellowship (2025-2027)** 
**EEG complexity and pain perception** <br>
Studying how measures of neural complexity in EEG signals relate to pain-related processing and pain perception.

## **DFF Research Project 1 (2027-2030)**  
**Pain in the patient's own words: From AI-based information-seeking to language-based assessment**  
Understanding the patient's perspective on pain, from the questions people ask AI to how their experiences can shape pain assessment.
