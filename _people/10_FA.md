## Bio and research interests

Fatemeh holds a bachelor's degree in Radiology (Medical Imaging) and a master's degree in Clinical Psychology. After working in different clinical imaging settings, she developed a growing interest in neuroscience and neuroimaging research. Her interdisciplinary background fuels her passion for integrating psychological perspectives with MRI to investigate the structural and functional organization of the brain, with a particular focus on pain perception.
In her free time, she enjoys traveling, swimming, cycling, dancing, and spending time on the volleyball court.
